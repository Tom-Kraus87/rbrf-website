    • Over the last 30 years, Jennifer handled matters for some of the largest insurance companies and their insureds in the country. She has an extensive trial history, having tried multiple cases to verdict in various district and circuit courts throughout Michigan. Throughout the years, Jennifer earned the reputation on both sides of the aisle as being a fair and honest litigator and an attorney that others can trust when evaluating a case for settlement.
