Our History

Ruggirello, Burke, Reizen & Fox, P.C. is a general practice and full-service law firm centrally located in the heart of historic downtown Mount Clemens, Michigan. Our firm is committed to providing the highest quality of services to all of our clients, while doing so in a timely and efficient manner at competitive rates.

Our clients include individuals, multinational corporations, new and emerging companies, insurance companies, municipal and government agencies, and privately held corporations.

Our firm draws on many years of experience with our notable partners in order to provide clients with the best personalized service possible.

The cornerstone of our firm’s success is that we listen to our clients’ needs and craft our representation on their behalf accordingly.
