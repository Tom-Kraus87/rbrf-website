Our History

Ruggirello, Burke, Reizen & Fox, P.C. is a general practice and full-service law firm centrally located in the heart of historic downtown Mount Clemens, Michigan. Our firm is committed to providing the highest quality of services to all of our clients, while doing so in a timely and efficient manner at competitive rates.

Our clients include individuals, multinational corporations, new and emerging companies, insurance companies, municipal and government agencies, and privately held corporations.

Our firm draws on many years of experience with our notable partners in order to provide clients with the best personalized service possible.

The cornerstone of our firm’s success is that we listen to our clients’ needs and craft our representation on their behalf accordingly.

Areas of Practice

Automobile Negligence

We help people who have been injured in car accidents recover the compensation they deserve. Whether it’s dealing with insurance companies or proving fault, our team handles the legal details so you can focus on healing.

First-Party No-Fault

If your own insurance company isn’t paying your medical bills or wage loss after an accident, we’ll step in to protect your rights. We work to make sure you receive the benefits your policy promises under no-fault insurance laws.



Insurance Coverage

Insurance policies can be confusing, especially when a claim is denied. We review your coverage, explain your options, and fight for the protection and payment you’re entitled to under your policy.




Commercial General Liability

When accidents or injuries happen in a business setting, we help companies and property owners navigate liability claims. Our goal is to resolve matters quickly and fairly, keeping your business protected.



Insurance Subrogation

We help recover money from the parties responsible for causing damage or loss, making sure the financial burden doesn’t fall unfairly on you or your insurer. Our team handles the process from investigation to recovery.

Home-Owners Liability

Accidents at home can lead to unexpected claims and stress. We guide homeowners through the legal and insurance process, ensuring your interests are protected and any claims are handled properly.

Property Loss

Whether it’s fire, water, or storm damage, property loss can be devastating. We help you navigate your insurance claim, address disputes, and work toward a full and fair recovery for your damages.

Facilitations/Arbitrations

Sometimes the best solutions happen outside the courtroom. We represent clients in mediation and arbitration to reach fair, cost-effective resolutions without the time and expense of a full trial.
